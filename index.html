IPhone

The IPhone is a line of touchscreen-based smartphones designed and marketed by Apple Inc. All generations of the IPhone use Apple's IOS mobile operating system software. The first-generation IPhone was releasedon June 29, 2007, and multiple new IOS releases have been released since.
